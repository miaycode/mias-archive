Fahrenheit 451

Author: Ray Bradbury

Finished: 2026-05-29

Honestly, I cant say I havent enjoyed this book. It was fresh and original, a first book Ive read in a while. The beginning started off really good, but then after Montags meetup with Faber, the plot went on too quickly for me. The end was quite boring for me and I had bigger expectations for it. Im disappointed that Clarisse was removed from the story so early on...
